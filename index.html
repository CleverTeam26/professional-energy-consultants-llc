FAQ

Questions about lowering your energy bill

How can nursing homes and assisted living facilities reduce their electricity bills in Texas?

By joining a group bidding program. Care facilities run around the clock, so their energy demand is high and steady. We combine the bills of many facilities into one large energy profile and ask suppliers to bid on it, which lets us negotiate rates that a single facility can't get on its own.

Upload your last three electric bills and we handle the bidding, with a goal of saving you up to 20%.

What is the benefit of group bidding for commercial energy procurement?

Group bidding combines the energy loads of many businesses into one larger contract. Suppliers compete harder for a large contract and offer lower rates than they would to one business. Smaller operations get the bulk-rate pricing usually reserved for large industrial companies.

How does the online utility bill upload portal work?

Upload your last three consecutive electric bills through our secure portal. Our consultants review your usage history and demand levels to estimate your savings, then bid your energy contract out to suppliers.

What types of businesses can benefit from commercial energy consulting?

We specialize in nursing homes and assisted living facilities across Texas and also work with micro-breweries. Any commercial or industrial business can benefit, especially high energy users, whether you run one location or many.

Does the energy audit cost anything?

No. The bill audit and savings review are free and come with no obligation to switch.

Which Texas cities do you serve?

We serve businesses across Texas, including Austin, Corpus Christi, Dallas, El Paso, Fort Worth, Houston, Lubbock, Plano, San Antonio and Temple.
